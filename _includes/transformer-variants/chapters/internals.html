Interpretability stance

Internal probes explain behavior; they do not replace behavioral evaluation

Validation loss tells us whether a model predicts held-out tokens. Throughput tells us how the implementation uses hardware. Internal probes answer a different kind of question: how broadly activations occupy representation space, how much one layer changes the geometry produced by the previous layer, and where individual attention heads place probability.

These diagnostics are descriptive evidence about trained checkpoints, not proof that one representation is intrinsically better. A high stable rank may reflect broad feature use or noise; a sharp CKA transition may indicate useful specialization or disruption. The interpretation becomes credible only when a probe is connected to the architecture and checked against quality and systems results.

Sparse expert behavior

Did the active-parameter-matched router use its stored capacity?

A top-two MoE can store many experts and still collapse onto a small subset. The utilization view measures each expert's share of selected routes by layer across five seeds. Router entropy supplies a second balance check. The stability view then asks whether independently trained seeds learn comparable routing decisions after expert labels are aligned on one half of the validation tokens and scored on the held-out half.

Representation dimensionality

Stable rank shows how activation energy spreads across directions

Stable rank is the squared Frobenius norm divided by the squared spectral norm. Informally, it becomes larger when activation energy is distributed across more singular directions instead of being dominated by one direction. It is a smooth effective-dimensionality statistic, not the exact algebraic rank.

Layer-to-layer change

CKA reveals where the representation geometry is preserved or reorganized

Linear centered kernel alignment compares the pairwise geometry of two activation matrices while remaining insensitive to isotropic scaling and orthogonal basis changes. High adjacent-layer CKA means two neighboring blocks preserve similar relationships among token representations; a low value marks a stronger geometric transition.

Head-level behavior

Compare reconstructed causal attention patterns

Memory-efficient kernels usually avoid retaining the full probability matrix. The exporter reconstructs exact pre-dropout softmax probabilities for selected checkpoints from the normalized layer input, applying the same RoPE rotations, ALiBI slopes, GQA head sharing, causal masks, and configured windows as the model. Each heatmap row is a query and each column is a possible key.

Engineering design

The system makes architectural claims auditable

The scientific comparison depends on software boundaries that keep variant knowledge explicit, recovery exact, and evaluation reproducible. These choices are part of the project outcome rather than invisible scaffolding.

01

Registry-driven construction

Problem. Scattered variant conditionals make it difficult to tell which components changed.

Choice. Each registered recipe declares its model class, position method, attention class, normalization, FFN, overrides, and optional per-layer pattern behind one build interface.

Consequence. The complete architecture matrix can be generated from explicit construction knowledge; a new recipe must declare its deviations instead of inheriting hidden trainer branches.

02

Protocol-level attention interface

Problem. Full attention, GQA, SWA, ALiBI, and linear recurrence expose different cache and visualization semantics.

Choice. Callers pass cache state opaquely and evaluators ask capabilities rather than assuming every operator materializes the same tensors.

Consequence. Unsupported cache and attention-map states remain visible, preventing a convenient but scientifically false common denominator.

03

Verified asynchronous checkpoints

Problem. GPU failures and partial writes can destroy progress or silently alter the run after recovery.

Choice. CPU snapshots, atomic replacement, SHA-256 verification, ring rotation, health monitoring, and restoration of model, optimizer, RNG, scheduler, and loader cursor.

Consequence. Recovery preserves the identity of a seed and its exact consumed-token trajectory, not only a set of model weights.

04

Frontend-agnostic evidence

Problem. A server-bound dashboard is fragile and difficult to preserve as a portfolio artifact.

Choice. Evaluators emit strict JSON and standalone interactive Plotly assets; this Jekyll site freezes a versioned snapshot and renders the same evidence with local, responsive controls.

Consequence. The page can be rebuilt without a Python server or chart CDN, while every displayed number remains traceable to the experiment commit.

Interpretation boundaries

What this evidence does not establish

Active parity is not stored-parameter parity

Full MoE activates 51.46M parameters per token versus Modern's 51.43M, but stores 103.37M because six experts are inactive for each token. The study fairly compares active computation while reporting the extra checkpoint and memory capacity explicitly.

Five seeds improve—not eliminate—uncertainty

Checkpoint seeds are the correct replication unit and five observations reduce standard error relative to the historical three-seed study. They still do not establish how rankings transfer to new corpora, parameter scales, or hardware.

Long context is extrapolation, not long-context training

Every recipe trains at 1,024 tokens and is tested up to 4,096. The result measures behavior outside the training range and should not be generalized to models trained natively at 4K.

SWA's stability is local

A 4K container does not imply a 4K receptive field. Each SWA query sees at most 256 tokens per layer, so flat perplexity cannot demonstrate retrieval from the distant prefix.

Throughput is hardware- and implementation-specific

Measurements come from an NVIDIA L4-24Q and these kernels, tensor shapes, cache paths, and batch sizes. The relative order can change with fused kernels or different accelerators.

Internal probes are diagnostic

Stable rank, CKA, and heatmaps reveal structure but do not identify a causal reason for downstream quality without targeted interventions and task-level probes.
